

AWS Global Infrastructure : 

Regions : a region is nothing but a physical or geographical location where AWS segregated the entire world into "39 regions" as of October 2026. 
India : Mumbai (ap-south-1).. Hyderbad (ap-south-2).. 

---

Availability zones / AZs : AZ consists of one or more discrete data centres. Every AZ will have its independent power, cooling, and physical security.  124 AZs as of 30 Oct..
Mumabi & Hyd regions has 3 AZs each..
Hyd: ap-south-2a , ap-south-2b & ap-south-2c

Actual workload runs within these availability zones. 

---

Point of presence / Edge Networks : as of today, October 2026, AWS has 750+ edge locations across the globe. 

CDN / Caching : 

TTL : time to live : Default : 86,400 Seconds (24 Hrs)... Max TTL: 365 Days.. 

---

Local Zones : These are like mini AWS data centres built in major metropolitan areas where AWS doesn't have a full region. 


----

Latency : Latency is nothing but the time delay between a cause and its effect. 

Time taken for a data packet to travel from its source to destination or to travel back (RTT - Round Trip Time)
