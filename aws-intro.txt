

AWS Global Infrastructure : 

Regions : a region is nothing but a physical or geographical location where AWS segregated the entire world into "39 regions" as of October 2026. 
India : Mumbai (ap-south-1).. Hyderbad (ap-south-2).. 

---

Availability zones / AZs : AZ consists of one or more discrete data centres. Every AZ will have its independent power, cooling, and physical security.  124 AZs as of 30 Oct..
Mumabi & Hyd regions has 3 AZs each..
Hyd: ap-south-2a , ap-south-2b & ap-south-2c

Actual workload runs within these availability zones. 

---

Point of presence / Edge Networks : as of today, October 2026, AWS has 750+ edge locations across the globe. 

CDN / Caching : 

TTL : time to live : Default : 86,400 Seconds (24 Hrs)... Max TTL: 365 Days.. 

---

Local Zones : These are like mini AWS data centres built in major metropolitan areas where AWS doesn't have a full region. 


----

Latency : Latency is nothing but the time delay between a cause and its effect. 

Time taken for a data packet to travel from its source to destination or to travel back (RTT - Round Trip Time)

Support Plans : 

1. Account and Billing related Queries : Activation, Service Activation, Billing info.. : Free for everyone
2. Technical Assistance : Any technical help from AWS : DEPENDS ON AWS SUPPORT PLAN..


Basic Support Plan : Free
- Included for every account automatically.
- 24/7 access to AI first troubleshooting agent, Amazon kiro-cli.. 
- Account and Billing related queries only.. NO Technical assistance from AWS team..
- limited trsuted advisor checks.. 

Business Support+ : From $29/month per account
- AWS recommended minimum plan for production workload
- 24/7 AI-first troubleshooting agent support
- 24/7 phone, chat, or email contact method with an "AWS support engineer"
- Critical case response is < 30 minutes
- Full Trusted Advisor checks
- Unlimited support cases from any user..

Enterprise Support Plan : From $5,000/month
- Recommended plan for production-critical workload
- A designated TAM (Technical account manager) will allocate.
- Critical case response is < 15 minutes.
- Full Trusted Advisor checks.
- Annual architectural reviews and operational reviews, along with Well Architected Framework review.
- Multi-account support (one support plan can be used with thousands of accounts).

Unified operations: From $50,000/month
- Recommended if you are running a mission-critical workload that requires enhanced resilience and an application-specific expert from AWS. 
- Everything from "Enterprise Support Plan"
- Critical case response is < 5 minutes.
- TAM + Domain Specialist + Incident Management
